Holiday Wheel Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

Holiday Wheel ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Summary: We collect minimal data, store everything locally on your device, and do not share any information with third parties.

Information We Collect

Holiday Wheel collects only the minimum information necessary for gameplay:

  • Display Names: Player names chosen during gameplay are stored locally on your device.
  • Game Session Data: Temporary game state (scores, puzzle progress) is stored in memory during play and is not persisted after the game ends.
  • Server URL: If you manually enter a game server address, it is stored locally for convenience.

Camera Usage (iOS App Only)

The Holiday Wheel Controller app requests camera access for one purpose only:

  • To scan QR codes displayed on the Apple TV screen for easy game joining.

No images or video are stored, transmitted, or processed beyond reading the QR code data. The camera is only active when you explicitly open the QR scanner screen.

Network Communication

The app communicates over your local WiFi network to synchronize game state between the Apple TV and player devices. This communication:

  • Stays within your local network
  • Does not transmit data to external servers
  • Contains only game-related information (scores, guesses, game state)

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your devices:

  • We do not operate cloud servers that store your data
  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs
  • Game data is cleared when you close the app

Third-Party Services

Holiday Wheel does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. We do not use:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media SDKs
  • Crash reporting services

Children's Privacy

Holiday Wheel is designed to be family-friendly and safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 or any other age group.

Data Retention

Since we don't collect data on external servers, there is no data retention policy needed. Local data on your device can be cleared by:

  • Deleting the app
  • Clearing app data in device settings

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the application.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
